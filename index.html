Step 1 of 4

Want to start tracking money?

Three short steps: opening balance, recurring income, then start recording. Everything can be changed later.

How it works Kantong records income and expenses as your personal cash flow. No bank account is connected and no data is shared.

You can add, rename, archive or delete any of them later in the Budget tab. Existing categories are never overwritten, and a category you delete does not come back.

If you choose Later Choosing “Later” does not lock the app. You can still record entries, and a reminder to finish the guide stays available.

First account Where is your money right now? Create your first account (e.g. BCA or Cash) and enter its balance today. You can add more accounts.

Your own record This number only drives the balance per your own records — it is not a verified bank balance.

Recurring income A salary or fixed income? Enter it once and Kantong records it automatically every month (once per month, including months you missed).

Short months Day 31 in a short month uses that month's last day. You can change or pause it later in the Recurring tab.
